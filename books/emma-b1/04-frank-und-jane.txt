# Frank Churchill und Jane Fairfax
= Frank Churchill and Jane Fairfax

In Highbury sprach man schon lange über Frank Churchill.
= In Highbury people had long been talking about Frank Churchill.
Er war der Sohn von Herrn Weston aus seiner ersten Ehe.
= He was Mr Weston's son from his first marriage.
Nach dem Tod seiner Mutter war er bei seinem reichen Onkel und seiner Tante Churchill aufgewachsen.
= After his mother's death he had grown up with his rich uncle and aunt, the Churchills.
Er trug auch ihren Namen.
= He also bore their name.
Seine Tante war oft krank und wollte ihn nie gehen lassen.
= His aunt was often ill and never wanted to let him go.
Deshalb hatte er seinen Vater noch nie in Highbury besucht.
= That is why he had never yet visited his father in Highbury.

Endlich, im Februar, kam er.
= At last, in February, he came.
Frank Churchill war dreiundzwanzig, gut aussehend, elegant und sehr charmant.
= Frank Churchill was twenty-three, handsome, elegant and very charming.
Er lachte gern, redete viel und machte jedem Komplimente.
= He liked to laugh, talked a lot and paid everyone compliments.
Vom ersten Tag an verbrachte er viel Zeit mit Emma.
= From the very first day he spent a lot of time with Emma.
Herr und Frau Weston freuten[sich freuen über|to be pleased about|#f] sich[#f] darüber.
= Mr and Mrs Weston were pleased about this.
Insgeheim hofften sie, dass die beiden eines Tages heiraten würden.
= Secretly they hoped that the two of them would marry one day.
Auch Emma gefiel Frank sehr. „Vielleicht bin ich ein bisschen in ihn verliebt“, dachte sie.
= Emma liked Frank very much too. "Perhaps I'm a little in love with him," she thought.

Zur selben Zeit kam auch Jane Fairfax nach Highbury.
= At the same time Jane Fairfax also came to Highbury.
Jane war die Nichte von Fräulein Bates, einer armen, freundlichen alten Jungfer, die ununterbrochen redete.
= Jane was the niece of Miss Bates, a poor, kind old spinster who talked without stopping.
Jane war so alt wie Emma, schön, elegant und sehr begabt. Sie spielte wunderbar Klavier.
= Jane was the same age as Emma, beautiful, elegant and very talented. She played the piano wonderfully.
Aber sie hatte kein Geld und musste bald als Gouvernante arbeiten.
= But she had no money and would soon have to work as a governess.
Emma mochte Jane nicht besonders. „Sie ist so kühl und so verschlossen“, sagte sie.
= Emma didn't particularly like Jane. "She is so cool and so reserved," she said.
Herr Knightley meinte: „Vielleicht bist du nur ein wenig eifersüchtig, weil sie so gut Klavier spielt.“
= Mr Knightley said: "Perhaps you're just a little jealous because she plays the piano so well."

Eines Tages bekam Jane ein wunderbares Geschenk: ein neues Klavier.
= One day Jane received a wonderful present: a new piano.
Niemand wusste, wer es geschickt hatte.
= No one knew who had sent it.
Emma hatte sofort eine Idee.
= Emma had an idea at once.
Jane hatte früher bei der Familie Campbell gewohnt, deren Tochter einen Herrn Dixon geheiratet hatte.
= Jane had once lived with the Campbell family, whose daughter had married a Mr Dixon.
„Bestimmt ist das Klavier von Herrn Dixon“, flüsterte[zuflüstern|to whisper (to someone)|#z] sie Frank zu[#z]. „Vielleicht ist er heimlich in Jane verliebt!“
= "The piano is surely from Mr Dixon," she whispered to Frank. "Perhaps he is secretly in love with Jane!"
Frank lachte. „Das ist gut möglich“, sagte er.
= Frank laughed. "That's quite possible," he said.
„Sie[Sie|you|formal "you" (always with a capital S)] haben so eine lebhafte Fantasie, Fräulein Woodhouse.“
= "You have such a lively imagination, Miss Woodhouse."
Später fand Emma diesen Klatsch nicht mehr so lustig.
= Later Emma no longer found this gossip so amusing.

Frau Weston hatte nämlich eine ganz andere Vermutung.
= Mrs Weston, you see, had quite a different guess.
„Vielleicht hat Herr Knightley das Klavier geschickt“, sagte sie. „Er hilft der Familie Bates ja oft. Vielleicht liebt er Jane!“
= "Perhaps Mr Knightley sent the piano," she said. "He often helps the Bates family, after all. Perhaps he loves Jane!"
Emma war darüber seltsam erschrocken.
= Emma was strangely alarmed by this.
„Herr Knightley darf nicht heiraten!“, rief sie. „Sonst bekommt mein kleiner Neffe Henry Donwell Abbey nicht!“
= "Mr Knightley must not marry!" she cried. "Otherwise my little nephew Henry won't get Donwell Abbey!"
Sie verstand selbst nicht, warum sie sich[#a] so aufregte[sich aufregen|to get upset|#a].
= She herself didn't understand why she was getting so upset.
