// Shared glossary for all French books.  form | lemma (empty = same as form) | English | note
// Every edition also has its own books/<edition>/glossary.txt, whose entries win over these.
// Words are split at elisions (l’ + homme, qu’ + il) and at pronouns joined by a hyphen
// (dit + il), so each part has its own entry. Keys use the typographic apostrophe ’.
// Nouns give the lemma with its article: la prison, le juge, l’homme (m).

// --- articles and small words ---
